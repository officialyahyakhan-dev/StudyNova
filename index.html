StudyNova Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the StudyNova mobile app (“StudyNova”, “the app”), developed by Yahya Khan (“we”, “us”), handles information. StudyNova is a study planner for students. It is built to work entirely on your device, without an account and without an internet connection.

In short: StudyNova does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

1. Information we collect

We collect no information. StudyNova has:

  • no user accounts or sign-in
  • no servers or cloud database
  • no analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • no advertising and no advertising IDs
  • no third-party SDKs that collect data

The app does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send your information anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, StudyNova saves what you enter in the app’s private storage on your device. This includes:

  • subjects, study goals and planned study sessions
  • tasks, assignments, checklists and deadlines
  • exams and syllabus topics
  • notes, tags and flashcards
  • habits and focus-timer history
  • your settings (theme, goals, reminder preferences)
  • an optional first name, used only to greet you on the Home screen

This information never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Other apps cannot read it.

3. When information leaves your device (only if you choose)

Your data only leaves your device when you take one of these actions yourself:

  • Exporting a backup. Tapping Export Backup creates a backup file and opens your phone’s share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Files, Google Drive or email).
  • Exporting notes or reports. Notes and weekly progress reports can be shared as PDF or text in the same way.
  • Restoring a backup. You pick a backup file using your phone’s file picker. StudyNova reads only the file you choose.

Once you share a file, the privacy policy of the service you chose (such as Google Drive or your email provider) applies to that copy.

Android’s automatic cloud backup is turned off for StudyNova, so your data is never copied to the cloud unless you export it.

5. How we use information

Information you enter is used only inside the app to provide its features: showing your schedule, calculating study time and streaks, sending the reminders you set, and creating exports you request. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information with anyone, because we never receive it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • delete individual items at any time in the app
  • erase everything with Settings → Erase All Data
  • uninstall StudyNova, which removes all of its data from your device

Backup or export files you have shared are not deleted by the app; you can delete them wherever you saved them.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server-side data that could be exposed. We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock and keeping exported backup files in a safe place.

9. Children’s privacy

StudyNova is suitable for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), because the app collects no personal information at all.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You remain in full control of your data on your device, as described in sections 3 and 7. If you have a question or request, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version with the app update. If a future version ever starts collecting data, we will tell you in the app before it happens.
